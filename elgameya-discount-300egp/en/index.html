Clear answers

Frequently asked questions about ElGameya

Answers based on the official website and recorded video tutorials. Service details can change.

How do I apply referral code ujrcxa?+

Open the ElGameya app through the referral link. If a referral or discount field appears, enter ujrcxa and verify any discount before confirming.

Is the 300 EGP discount guaranteed?+

No guarantee is stated here. The page owner provided the 300 EGP figure; it is not independently confirmed by a public official announcement. Eligibility and current terms appear in the app.

What durations are available?+

The official FAQ describes 3-, 6- and 10-month groups, subject to availability when registering.

How much are the group and monthly installments?+

The official FAQ lists group totals from EGP 5,000 to EGP 100,000 and monthly installments from EGP 500 to EGP 10,000. Options depend on your account.

How can I pay installments?+

Officially listed options include debit and credit cards, bank transfers, mobile wallets, Fawry and OPay. Confirm current fees in the app.

When are installments paid and payouts received?+

The official FAQ lists payment windows from the 25th to the 3rd and payouts from the 15th to the 25th of the selected month. Check your agreement.

How do payout-turn fees work?+

Fees can vary according to group duration and your payout turn. Review the amounts quoted in the app before signing.

What happens if I pay late or withdraw?+

Contractual fees or obligations may apply. Read your current agreement and consult official ElGameya support before taking action.

Read the current agreement, fees and payment dates before joining any savings group. This page is for information only, not financial advice or a guarantee.
